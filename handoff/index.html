Sales to Production Handoff.

What the office needs from you before a job can be released to production — and what happens if it's missing.

Policy

A job can move into production only after every required checklist item is complete and uploaded to the correct job in JobNimbus. Commission is paid only after the job has moved into production.

Project type
What the office needs 0 of 4 checked

This is your own self-check. Checking a box here does not change a JobNimbus status, submit the job, or mean the office has approved it.

If the file is incomplete

The office will return the job to the salesperson with the items that need correction. Update the JobNimbus record and send it back for review. The job stays out of production, and commission stays on hold, until the handoff is complete and the job has moved into production.
