Section 01

Ideal's workmanship warranty

We stand behind the work we do. If an issue is caused by how our team installed, prepared, or applied something, we'll correct the affected work during the applicable warranty period.

5 years

Roofing, gutter installation, siding installation, and exterior painting. Coverage begins when the installation or painting is complete.

3 years

Exterior staining, including eligible deck and fence surfaces. Coverage begins when staining is complete.

What does workmanship cover?

Coverage depends on what caused the issue. For example, if a leak traces back to flashing we installed incorrectly, it falls under our roofing workmanship warranty during the five-year term. Damage caused by hail, wind, impact, or someone disturbing the finished work falls outside that coverage. One example is a later contractor damaging the flashing while installing equipment.

Finish exclusion · paint & stain only

Which painted or stained surfaces are excluded?

Ideal's workmanship warranties exclude paint and stain finishes on flat, horizontal surfaces. That includes deck floors and boards, stair treads, landings, and the flat tops of railings. Walking and shoveling wear down deck and stair finishes, while rail tops take direct exposure to sun, snow, and standing moisture. On the same deck, stained vertical posts and balusters are eligible for the three-year workmanship warranty.

Section 02

Owens Corning roofing warranties

Ideal provides the workmanship coverage above. On qualifying roofing projects, Owens Corning also provides coverage under the warranty issued for that roof. Check the job's warranty paperwork before quoting a tier to a homeowner.

These highlights apply to qualifying limited-lifetime shingles on single-family homes. The installed products, property type, and warranty issued for the job determine the actual coverage.

Standard Product Limited Warranty

Product defects

For qualifying shingles, the initial TRU PROtection period is 10 years. After that, coverage for a covered manufacturing defect is limited to prorated materials.

Workmanship

This tier includes no Owens Corning workmanship coverage. Ideal's five-year roofing workmanship warranty still applies.

What is TRU PROtection?

TRU PROtection is the initial period when Owens Corning covers eligible materials and installation labor to address a covered manufacturing defect. A workmanship claim concerns an error in how the roof was installed.
